Selected project / Residential

House
No. 17

Dhaka / 2026
A study in
quietness.

House No. 17 explores how a restrained material palette, controlled daylight and layered thresholds can create a home that feels open without losing intimacy.

Rather than relying on one dramatic gesture, the design builds atmosphere through proportion, framed views, warm surfaces and a sequence of spaces that become gradually more private.
