About Moiré

A studio
for space.

Architecture and interiors with a calm point of view and an obsession with the details that shape experience.

01 / About usStudio / Dhaka
Moiré is an architecture and interior design studio interested in making spaces that feel inevitable — considered, contextual and quietly expressive.

We work across residential, hospitality and commercial environments, developing ideas from the scale of a building to the smallest material junction.

Our studio values clarity over spectacle. We believe a successful space should feel natural to occupy, while still carrying a strong sense of identity.

Our approach
Think deeply.
Build carefully.

We bring together spatial planning, material thinking and a close attention to detail. The ambition is simple: create work that remains relevant after the first impression.
