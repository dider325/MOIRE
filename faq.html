FAQQuestions / Answers
A few useful answers before we begin.
What does Moiré design? +

We work across architecture and interiors, from residential spaces to commercial environments. Our role can include concept, planning, design development and material direction.

How does a project begin? +

We begin with a conversation around the brief, site, goals, constraints and desired experience. From there, we define a clear scope and design direction.

Can you work with an existing building? +

Yes. Existing conditions, adaptive reuse and renovation can all be part of the design process.

Do you handle interiors as well as architecture? +

Yes. We can work across architecture and interiors so the overall design language remains coherent from structure to finish.

What should I send for an initial enquiry? +

A short project description, location, approximate size and your goals are enough to start a useful conversation.
